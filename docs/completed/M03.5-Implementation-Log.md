### Decisions and deviations

1. **Completed groups by status, not type.** A completed adventure of any type goes to "Completed", so "Main story" shows the storyline still in play. D12 did not say where a completed main adventure goes.
2. **"Save mine anyway"** needs no service flag: the surface saves again against the latest `updated_at` that `AdventureConflictError.latest` carries.
3. **Errors from objective and update writes** that find no row refetch the adventure instead of dropping it, since the adventure may still exist. Adventure-level writes drop it, as §6.3 says.
4. **Helper reuse.** `adventureRules.ts` imports `relativeTime` and `noteAuthorLabel` from `notes/noteRules.ts`; the copy is identical, so nothing was duplicated.
5. The character-delete confirmation count (D5) and the Delete campaign copy are Phase 4, as planned.
